A seller on r/homelabsales is listing CloudGenix ION 3000 1U appliances — brand new — for just $45 each. That's an Atom C2758 (8-core), 8GB RAM, a 240GB SSD and a frankly absurd 14 GbE ports in a proper rack-mount chassis.

These are ex-Palo Alto SD-WAN boxes. Once their cloud licensing becomes irrelevant, the underlying hardware is a perfectly capable x86 appliance. Flash pfSense, OPNsense or VyOS onto it and you've got a router/firewall with more NICs than most people know what to do with — VLANs, dedicated management ports, inline IDS taps, the lot.

Why homelabbers should care

Fourteen copper gigabit ports at this price is almost unheard of. Comparable new mini-PCs with 4–6 ports from the usual suspects cost three to five times as much. If you're building out network segmentation, running Suricata inline, or just want a beefy multi-WAN gateway, this ticks a lot of boxes.

Caveats

The Atom C2758 is from the Rangeley family — yes, the generation with the infamous clock signal bug (AVR54). Many boards were revised or had mitigations applied, but it's worth asking the seller about hardware revision. The chip is also getting long in the tooth; don't expect it to push line-rate with heavy Snort rules, though for most home connections it'll be fine. This is a US-only sale, so UK readers will need to factor in shipping and import duty — still likely a bargain if you can arrange forwarding, but it eats into the savings.

Also worth noting: stock on listings like this goes fast. If it's gone by the time you read this, keep an eye on r/homelabsales — these ex-enterprise SD-WAN boxes pop up periodically.
