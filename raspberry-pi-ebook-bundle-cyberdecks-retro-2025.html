Humble Bundle has teamed up with Raspberry Pi Press again for a pay-what-you-want e-book bundle covering cyberdecks, retro gaming, and assorted Pi projects. If you've been eyeing up a new build or just want some weekend reading, this is a genuinely cheap way to stock up on official guides.

What's in it?

The bundle includes books on building cyberdecks, retro gaming setups, and various maker projects — all from the official Raspberry Pi Press. You pick your tier, pay a few quid, and get DRM-free PDFs. A portion goes to charity.

Full details are over at the Raspberry Pi blog.

Why homelabbers should care

Pi boards are everywhere in homelabs — running Pi-hole, Home Assistant, network monitors, you name it. But cyberdecks and portable builds are genuinely useful too. A self-contained terminal with a Pi 5 inside makes a solid field diagnostics tool for your rack, or a portable SSH client when you're working on headless gear.

The retro gaming guides are less directly relevant, but let's be honest — half the fun of a homelab is the side projects.

Caveats

These are Humble Bundle books, so the quality varies. Some are excellent step-by-step guides, others are more inspirational "look what people built" collections. Check the tier contents before you pay up to the top level. Also, these bundles are time-limited — they don't hang around forever, so grab it if you want it.

And as always with Pi projects, the books are the cheap bit. The components, screens, and cases add up fast.
